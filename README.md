# Nefas Dei

**Independent ideas. Open collaboration. Things worth building.**

Nefas Dei is an independent space for building software, exploring technology, and turning interesting ideas into working projects.

There isn't a single specialty or a fixed direction here. We experiment with different technologies and work across different areas, from everyday software and developer tools to artificial intelligence and whatever else catches our curiosity.

Some projects might solve practical problems. Others might simply explore what's possible.

The common thread is simple: **an idea is worth exploring.**

---

### What we're building

A growing collection of independent projects, experiments, and open-source software.

- **Software & Applications** — Tools and applications built to be useful, practical, or simply interesting.
- **Developer Tools** — Experiments and utilities that make development a little easier.
- **Artificial Intelligence** — Exploring machine learning, AI applications, and intelligent systems.
- **Experiments** — Ideas that don't fit into a category yet. Some might become projects; others might remain experiments.

Not everything starts with a roadmap. Sometimes it starts with curiosity.

### Want to build something with us?

Nefas Dei is currently an independent initiative, but that doesn't mean it has to stay that way.

We're open to meeting people who enjoy experimenting, learning, and building things together.

Whether you're a developer, designer, researcher, or someone with an interesting idea, there's room to get involved.

You can contribute by:

- Exploring projects and submitting improvements.
- Reporting bugs or suggesting new features.
- Sharing ideas and starting discussions.
- Collaborating on something entirely new.

You don't need to know everything or be an expert in every technology. Curiosity and a willingness to contribute are a good place to start.

**Interested?** Explore the repositories, open an issue, or reach out.

---

### Our approach

- Build things that are useful, interesting, or both.
- Stay curious and explore beyond familiar technologies.
- Keep things open and encourage collaboration.
- Learn by building, experimenting, and sharing.
